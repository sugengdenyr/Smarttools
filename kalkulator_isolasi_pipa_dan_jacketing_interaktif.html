Rincian Perhitungan Isolasi Pipa Lembaran (Excel Match)

Formulasi Presisi SPINDO
• Total Panjang & Keliling Pipa 120 Meter | 0.528 m

Total Panjang Pipa = 6 m × 20 btg = 120 Meter

Keliling Pipa = Phi (π) × Dia. Pipa OD = 3.14 × 168.3 mm = 528.46 mm = 0.528 Meter

• Luas Penampang Pipa (Keliling × Panjang) 63.41 m²

Luas Penampang Pipa = 0.528 m × 120 m = 63.41 m²

• Kebutuhan Isolasi Lembaran (Armaflex) & Spare 23.25 Lembar

Luas 1 Lembar Isolasi = 1 m × 3 m = 3 m²

Penampang / Luas Isolasi = 63.41 m² / 3 m² = 21.14 Lembar

Isolasi Dibutuhkan (+ Spare 10%) = 21.14 × 1.1 = 23.25 Lembar

• Perhitungan Stapping (Skrup Pengunci Jacketing Memanjang) 1200 Pcs

Jumlah Stapping = Total Panjang Pipa (120 m) × 10 pcs/m = 1200 Pcs
