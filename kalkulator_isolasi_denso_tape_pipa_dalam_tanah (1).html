Formulasi Perhitungan Lilitan Denso Tape Spiral (Geometri Akurat)

Geometri Presisi
• 1. Keliling Pipa & Langkah Pitch Lilitan (Effective Pitch) C = 0.359m | Pitch = 50mm

Keliling Pipa ($C$) = $\pi \times \text{OD} = 3.14 \times 114.3 \text{ mm} = 358.90 \text{ mm} = 0.359\text{ Meter}$.

Langkah Maju Pitch ($P$) = $\text{Lebar Tape} - \text{Overlap} = 100\text{mm} - 50\text{mm} = 50\text{ mm}$.

• 2. Kebutuhan Panjang Denso Tape Per Meter Pipa 7.25 m Tape / m Pipa

Panjang Tape per 1 Lilitan ($L_{\text{turn}}$) = $\sqrt{C^2 + P^2} = \sqrt{358.9^2 + 50^2} = 362.37\text{ mm}$.

Faktor Lilitan Spiral = $L_{\text{turn}} / P = 362.37 / 50 = 7.25 \text{ meter tape / meter pipa}$.

• 3. Total Kebutuhan Denso Tape & Kebutuhan Roll Order 173.9 Meter | 18 Roll

Total Tape Akurat = $\text{Panjang Pipa} \times \text{Faktor Lilitan} = 24\text{m} \times 7.25 = 173.9\text{ Meter}$.

Jumlah Order BPB = $\lceil 173.9 / 10\text{m} \rceil = 18 Roll$.
